## 4. Deployment is closed *in the package* and open *in the runtime* — the distinction is load-bearing

Step 16 provides every deployment gate a release needs, and this lane confirms the prompts exist
and are complete. **That is a package-level finding and nothing more.** In the runtime:

- **`RGL-BLK-001` remains architecturally unfixed** — production auto-deploys from `main` with no
  go/no-go gate. Step 16's `404_GO_NO_GO_REPORT` gate exists as a prompt and is defeated in
  practice by the deployment mechanism it is meant to govern.
- **`UAT_ACCEPTED` was never obtained** — `400_UAT_DEPLOYMENT` exists; no UAT environment and no
  named acceptor does.
- **No staging tier exists.** `399_STAGING_DEPLOYMENT` exists as a prompt; the tier does not.

**No finding is raised against the package for these** — the prompts are present and correct,
and a prompt cannot be blamed for an environment. They are recorded here because a reader of a
"Deployment: CLOSED" row would otherwise reasonably infer something false.
