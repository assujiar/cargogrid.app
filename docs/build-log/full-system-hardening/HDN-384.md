CASCADE` cannot cascade outward into Supabase-managed schemas — the `auth.users` collision-clearing step remains necessary and sufficient. **Completeness gaps found**: Prompt 384's own 5-outage-class charter (database/file/provider/job/security) was silently narrowed to 4 scenarios with no explicit disclosure of the mapping decision (fixed — a disclosure sentence added to `disaster-recovery.md` §1); `HARDENING_MATRIX.md` §16 (`HDN-385`) did not forward-link any of this checkpoint's own durable findings, and its own "Upstream" field skipped `HDN-384` entirely (fixed — a new dimension row and updated Upstream field added); `app.files` has now gone unexercised in a DR/backup drill's seeded slice across 2 consecutive checkpoints, always disclosed inline, never tracked as its own finding (fixed — registered as `ISS-2026-268`). `ISS-2026-258` and `ISS-2026-251` found related but not duplicate — cross-referenced rather than merged.
